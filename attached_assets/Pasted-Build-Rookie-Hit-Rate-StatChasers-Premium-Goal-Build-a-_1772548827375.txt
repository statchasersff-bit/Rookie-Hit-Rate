Build “Rookie Hit Rate” (StatChasers Premium)

Goal: Build a StatChasers-branded web tool that analyzes the last 10+ years of dynasty rookie drafts and shows hit rates by position × rookie draft round × year, where a “hit” means a player has recorded at least one Top-12 / Top-24 / Top-36 fantasy season at their position since entering the NFL. Also calculate time-to-breakout (how many seasons after entering the league their first hit occurred). Provide filters for format (1QB/SF) + scoring (PPR/HPPR/STD) and outcome definitions (Elite/Starter/Flex/Bust). The UX should feel premium and match StatChasers branding.

4) Core Definitions + Computations
A) “Hit” logic

A player is a “hit” if they have at least one season after rookie year where:

Games >= min_games

Position rank in that season <= threshold:

Elite: <= 12

Starter: <= 24

Flex: <= 36 (RB/WR only; for QB/TE treat as <=24 unless user selects otherwise)

Bust:

No seasons meeting chosen threshold within max_years_considered (default 6)

B) Breakout year

For each player:

Find first season (chronologically) where they qualify as a hit.

Breakout time = first_hit_season - rookie_year + 1
Example: rookie year 2021, first hit 2023 → breakout time = 3

C) Cohort aggregation

Group by:

position

rookie_round

dynasty_year (optional)
Within group compute:

hit_rate = hits / total

bust_rate = busts / total

starter_rate, elite_rate etc.

median_breakout among hitters

hit_by_year: % who hit by year 1, year 2, year 3+

D) Confidence indicator

Compute Wilson interval (or simple binomial CI) for hit_rate and expose it.
UI:

If interval width is large, show a “low confidence” icon.
